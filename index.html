Offline *99# Setup Guide

Complete step-by-step registration & usage guide
1

Register Mobile Number with Bank

Ensure your SIM card is registered with your bank account for UPI services.

2

Dial *99# from your SIM

Open phone dialer, type *99# and press Call using your registered GSM SIM (Airtel, VI, BSNL).

3

Select Your Bank Name

Type your bank name or first 3 letters (e.g., "SBI" or "HDF") to link your bank account.

4

Set Up UPI PIN

Enter last 6 digits of debit card & expiry date to create your 4 or 6-digit UPI PIN.

5

Send Money Offline

Select Option 1 (Send Money), enter recipient UPI ID or Phone, amount, and your UPI PIN.

Jio SIM Note: Reliance Jio 4G/5G is a VoLTE-only data network without 2G/3G GSM voice channel, so *99# USSD is restricted on Jio SIM cards. Use Airtel, VI, or BSNL for *99# USSD.
