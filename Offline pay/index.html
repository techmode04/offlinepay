Help & Setup Guide

1. One-Time USSD Setup

Dial *99# from your registered SIM card (Airtel, VI, BSNL). Select your bank name and set up your 4 or 6-digit UPI PIN.

2. How to Pay Offline

Scan any merchant UPI QR code using OffPay or enter recipient UPI ID. OffPay will launch your phone dialer with *99# pre-filled.

Reliance Jio SIM Notice

Jio VoLTE networks do not support *99# USSD service. Please dial from an Airtel, VI, or BSNL SIM card, or use offline QR voucher.
